# 📚 ExamPrep AI

> Your study material, turned into a smarter way to learn.

ExamPrep AI is an AI-powered academic tutor designed to help students understand, practice, and revise their study material in one place.

Instead of simply summarizing a PDF or set of notes, ExamPrep AI transforms study material into structured notes, simple Hinglish explanations, exam-ready answers, practice questions, quizzes, flashcards, and quick revision resources.

---

## ✨ Why ExamPrep AI?

Studying from long PDFs, handwritten notes, and scattered study material can be overwhelming.

ExamPrep AI follows a simple approach:

**Upload → Understand → Practice → Test → Revise**

It helps students move from simply *reading* a topic to actually *understanding and remembering* it.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📚 Structured Notes
Convert uploaded study material into:

- Topic-wise notes
- Important definitions
- Key concepts
- Formulas
- Examples
- Important points

### 🧠 Hinglish Understanding
Complex concepts are explained in simple, natural Hinglish.

The goal isn't to translate the notes word-for-word, but to explain them like a teacher would explain them to a student.

### 🧑‍🎓 Beginner Mode
Difficult concepts can be broken down into simple explanations for students who are learning the topic from scratch.

### 🎯 Exam-Focused Learning
Identify important concepts and prepare content according to exam requirements.

Generate:

- 2-mark answers
- 5-mark answers
- 10-mark answers

### ❓ Practice Questions
Generate different types of questions from the study material:

- Multiple Choice Questions
- Short Answer Questions
- Conceptual Application Questions

### 🧪 Topic Quizzes
Test your understanding after studying a topic and get immediate feedback with explanations.

### 🃏 Flashcards
Turn important concepts into interactive flashcards for quick revision.

### ⚡ Quick Revision
Get a concise revision sheet containing:

- Important definitions
- Key concepts
- Formulas
- Keywords
- Must-remember points

### 🔄 Smart Revision
Different revision modes for different situations:

- 5-Minute Revision
- Exam Revision
- Last-Minute Revision

### 📊 Progress Tracking
Keep track of:

- Topics completed
- Quiz performance
- Revision progress
- Weak areas

### 🧩 Weak Topic Detection
Identify concepts where you are making repeated mistakes and focus your revision on them.

### 💬 Ask a Doubt
Students can ask questions about their study material and get explanations grounded in the provided content.

### 🔍 Important Keywords
Automatically identify important terms and provide simple explanations for them.

### 🖼️ Visual Explanations
Concepts that are easier to understand visually can be represented using simple diagrams and flowcharts.

### 🎤 Listen & Learn
Hinglish explanations are written in a natural format that can work well with text-to-speech.
